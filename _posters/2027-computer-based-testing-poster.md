excerpt: 'Widely available AI coding tools have forced educators to rethink
traditional assessments for programming courses, especially for
debugging skills. Our team of instructors for a systems program-
ming course have implemented two debugging assessments (coding
interviews and computer-based testing) with the goal of incentiviz-
ing student learning and obtaining accurate evaluations of student
mastery. We present data collected across two course offerings
that summarize student and staff experiences with the assessments.
Additionally, we compare the strengths and drawbacks of each ap-
proach and make recommendations for successful implementation
of each.'
